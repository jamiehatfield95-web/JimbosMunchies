Our Story

Jimbo's Munchies was created in honor of my late father, Jim, a proud Army veteran who loved homemade cookies, late-night munchies, and spending time with family. Every cookie and every protein energy oatmeal ball is homemade with love using quality ingredients. Thank you for supporting our small family business and helping keep Jim's memory alive.
